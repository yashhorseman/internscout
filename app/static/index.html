InternScout

A student project. It searches postings collected from the public job boards of a limited set of companies; check the posting before applying. Messages are stored to improve the tool and deleted after 90 days, or sooner if you ask whoever shared this link. They are also sent to a free AI service, which may use them to improve its products, so please do not type personal or sensitive details. This browser keeps your conversation id; clearing site data forgets it.
