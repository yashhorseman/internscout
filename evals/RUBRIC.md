# Labeling rubric

Grade each posting against the query **and its filters**, from what the posting says.

| Grade | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 2 | Clearly what the query asks for: right kind of work, level and place |
| 1 | Partly: right field, but the level or place is off; or the right level in a neighbouring field |
| 0 | Does not match |

## Edge rules
- A senior, staff, lead or manager role is never above 0 for an intern or entry-level query.
- For a query naming a city, the right role in another Indian city is at most 1; in another country, 0.
- A query naming a company: a posting from any other company is 0.
- "Remote" means the posting says remote. Hybrid is at most 1.
- A skill query: 2 needs the skill to be part of the job, not only listed as a nice extra.
- If you cannot tell from what is shown, give the lower grade.
- A "no answer" query is expected to get mostly 0. Do not be generous to fill it.
